Research

Relationships.
Health. Social context.

I study how social relationships shape health and well-being, and how aging and life transitions reshape those relationships.

Drawing on longitudinal studies, multinational surveys, and sociocentric network data.

Social connections and inequality

What makes a social connection meaningful?

I examine how the structure and reciprocity of social relationships relate to well-being, and how these relationships differ across social and national contexts.

KSHAP

Reciprocal ties, loneliness, and well-being

Distinguishing mutual, outgoing-only, and incoming-only nominations to understand how different forms of connection relate to loneliness and well-being.

ISSP · 30 countries

Social capital and life satisfaction

Examining how the association between network resources and life satisfaction varies with individual income, national affluence, and inequality.

KSHAP

Social participation and network change

Studying the co-evolution of discussion networks, social participation, and depressive symptoms, with attention to selection, influence, and shared circumstances.

02 /

Health and aging

How are social relationships and health intertwined?

My work connects oral health, social connectedness, and mental and cognitive health in later life, using longitudinal data and multiple perspectives on social ties.

KSHAP

Oral health, received ties, and cognitive aging

Examining received discussion nominations as a potential pathway between tooth loss and later cognitive function, alongside self-reported ties.

KSHAP

Trajectories of social connectedness

Tracing how outgoing ties, their geographic reach, and incoming nominations contribute to longitudinal network patterns, with cognition as an external criterion.

KSHAP

Tooth loss, social connections, and loneliness

Examining whether the association between tooth loss and subsequent loneliness varies with nominations received from people other than a spouse.

03 /

Family and the life course

How do relationships change as lives unfold?

I study family connections and changing social networks through midlife and later life, including intergenerational support and periods of wider social disruption.

KLoEE

Intergenerational support in the sandwich generation

Examining configurations of contact, coresidence, and financial support to parents and adult children, and their links to subsequent depressive symptoms and self-esteem.

KLoSA · 2018–2022

Social network transitions around COVID-19

Following changes in network types before and during the pandemic and examining their association with later depressive symptoms.
