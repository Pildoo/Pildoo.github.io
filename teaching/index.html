University teaching and methods workshops connecting sociological theory, empirical research, health, aging, and inequality.

I teach courses that connect sociological theory and empirical research with questions about health, aging, social relationships, and inequality. Across university courses and methods workshops, I emphasize how to turn broad social questions into testable claims and evidence-based arguments.
