How to join the Social Connections & Health Lab at Hanyang University as a doctoral, master's, or undergraduate research student.

Social Connections & Health Lab

Join the lab

대학원생·학부연구생 모집 안내

The lab studies aging, health, and social relationships with Korean panel and whole-community network data. We are looking for students who want to learn how to turn a real question about later life into evidence. 본 연구실은 국내 패널자료와 완전연결망 자료로 노화·건강·사회적 관계를 연구합니다. 노년기에 대한 실제 질문을 근거 있는 분석으로 바꾸는 법을 배우고자 하는 학생을 찾습니다.

Who we look for

Three ways to join

지원 경로

Students come to the lab from sociology, but also from public health, social welfare, gerontology, and statistics. What matters is a real question you care about and the willingness to learn the methods it takes to answer it. 사회학뿐 아니라 보건학, 사회복지학, 노년학, 통계학 배경의 학생도 함께합니다. 중요한 것은 스스로 관심 있는 질문과, 그 질문에 답하기 위한 방법을 배우려는 자세입니다.

Doctoral

박사과정

Doctoral members develop an independent line of work inside the lab's program—framing a question, designing the analysis, and carrying it through to publication—while contributing to ongoing projects.

연구실의 연구 프로그램 안에서 독립적인 연구 주제를 발전시키고, 진행 중인 과제에도 함께 참여합니다.

Master's

석사과정

Master's members build a working foundation in panel and network analysis, take part in an ongoing project, and write a thesis on a question of their own.

패널·연결망 분석의 기초를 다지면서 진행 중인 연구에 참여하고, 자신의 주제로 학위논문을 씁니다.

Undergraduate

학부 연구생

Undergraduates join for a semester or longer to learn data cleaning, measurement, and basic analysis by working alongside graduate members. It is a good way to find out whether research suits you before applying to graduate school.

한 학기 이상 참여하며 자료 정리와 기초 분석을 배웁니다. 대학원 진학 전에 연구가 자신에게 맞는지 확인해 볼 수 있는 기회입니다.

Preparation

What helps before you start

준비하면 좋은 것

None of this is a prerequisite—members learn most of it in the lab. It is a map of where you will end up, not a gate you have to pass first. 아래 항목은 필수 자격 요건이 아니라 연구실에서 함께 익혀 가는 내용입니다.

Coursework수강하면 좋은 과목

  • Social Networks and Social Capital (SOC9115) Graduate seminar covering the network concepts the lab's projects are built on.
  • Medical Sociology Seminar (SOC9116) Graduate seminar on the social determinants of health and illness.
  • Intermediate Social Statistics (SOC9109) Regression and the statistical grounding the lab's longitudinal models assume.

Skills익혀 두면 좋은 기술

  • One statistical package, reasonably well Stata or R is enough to begin; the lab uses both, plus Mplus for latent variable models.
  • Reading empirical articles closely Being able to follow how a paper moves from a question to a design to a table matters more than knowing every technique.
  • English academic reading and writing The lab publishes in international journals, so most reading and writing is in English.
How to apply

Getting in touch

지원 방법

Graduate admission runs through the Department of Sociology's regular admissions cycle, but the conversation should start earlier. Email before you apply so we can talk about whether your questions and the lab's work fit each other. 대학원 입학은 사회학과의 정규 입학 전형을 따르지만, 그 전에 먼저 이메일로 연락 주시기를 권합니다. 관심 주제와 연구실의 연구가 서로 맞는지 미리 이야기해 보는 편이 좋습니다.

  1. Write to Pildoo Sung

    Send an email to pildoosung@hanyang.ac.kr with "Lab inquiry" and your intended track (doctoral, master's, or undergraduate research) in the subject line.

  2. Include four things

    • A short statement—two or three paragraphs—on what you want to study and why this lab.
    • Your CV or résumé.
    • A transcript, with statistics and methods courses noted.
    • Optionally, a writing sample: a thesis, term paper, or research report.

  3. Talk it through

    If there is a good fit, we will meet—in person or online—to discuss possible questions, data, and what the next year or two would actually look like.

  4. Apply to the department

    Formal admission is decided by the graduate program, not the lab. Check the Department of Sociology for the current cycle, required documents, and deadlines.

On funding and research assistantships. Whether funded positions are open depends on the projects running at the time. Ask in your first email and you will get a straight answer.

연구비 지원과 연구보조원(RA) 자리는 그 시점에 진행 중인 과제에 따라 달라집니다. 첫 이메일에서 함께 문의해 주시면 솔직하게 답변드리겠습니다.

Not sure whether your question fits? Write anyway—that is usually the fastest way to find out.
