Current program

What we work on

연구 주제

Our current program uses sociocentric—whole-community—network data that repeatedly record who names whom among older residents of rural Korean townships. Because everyone in the community is observed, we can separate the ties people report from the nominations they receive, which egocentric surveys cannot do. 현재 연구실의 주요 과제는 농촌 지역 노인 전수를 대상으로 “누가 누구를 지목했는가”를 반복 조사한 완전연결망(sociocentric) 자료를 활용합니다. 지역사회 구성원 전체를 관찰하기 때문에, 본인이 보고한 관계와 다른 사람들로부터 받은 지목을 구분할 수 있습니다.

01

Being chosen and loneliness받는 지목과 외로움

Do the nominations older adults receive—especially from non-kin—protect against later loneliness beyond the ties they name themselves, and does that protection differ in advanced old age?

02

Selection or influence선택인가 영향인가

When does similarity between connected villagers reflect choosing similar others, and when does it reflect influence? Stochastic actor-oriented models compare visible behaviors—social participation, drinking, smoking—with less visible states such as depressive symptoms as networks and behavior co-evolve.

03

From teeth to ties구강건강에서 관계망으로

How does tooth loss change the discussion nominations older adults receive, and what does that mean for later cognitive function?

04

Network trajectories관계망 궤적

How do received and sent ties change across survey waves, and how are those long-run trajectories linked to cognitive aging?

05

Cultural holes문화적 구조적 공백

What does it mean for well-being when older adults bridge culturally dissimilar people who are not otherwise connected? This project builds culturally weighted measures of brokerage to find out.

Data and methods

사용하는 자료와 방법

We work with long-running Korean panel studies and cross-national surveys, and we treat methods training as part of the lab's everyday work. Members learn the tools on live projects rather than in the abstract. 국내 장기 패널조사와 국제 비교조사 자료를 함께 사용하며, 방법론 훈련을 연구실 일상의 일부로 봅니다. 진행 중인 연구에 참여하면서 분석 방법을 익힙니다.

Data자료

  • KSHAP Whole-community network panels of older adults in rural Korean townships—the backbone of our sociocentric work.
  • KLoSA Korean Longitudinal Study of Ageing: a nationally representative panel of older Koreans.
  • Other panels and surveys KLoEE and SAFETRIP, plus cross-national surveys such as the ISSP and CFPS for comparative work.

Methods분석 방법

  • Network models Stochastic actor-oriented models for the co-evolution of networks and behavior, plus brokerage and centrality measures.
  • Typologies and change Latent class and latent transition analysis, and group-based trajectory modeling.
  • Longitudinal and dyadic designs Multi-wave panel models, fixed and random effects, and caregiver–care recipient dyadic models.

Software분석 도구

Most analysis runs in Stata and R, with Mplus for latent variable models. New members are not expected to arrive fluent in all three.
